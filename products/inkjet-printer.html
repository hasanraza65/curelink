CP-100 Inkjet Printer

Conveyor coding for dates, batches and barcodes

25 m/minTouchscreen100–240 V

Conveyor coding for dates, batches and barcodes

An automated transmission and coding machine: packs travel along the built-in conveyor while the inkjet head prints expiry dates, batch numbers and barcodes onto them.

Built for the end of a packing line rather than a desk. Packs are fed onto the conveyor by hand or automatically, carried past the print head, and coded as they pass — the practical way to apply manufacturing dates, expiry dates and batch numbers to cartons, pouches and labels.

Key features

What sets it apart

  • Integrated conveyor A built-in conveyor belt supporting both automatic and manual feed, running at up to 25 m/min.
  • Two print heights Available with a 12.7 mm or 25.4 mm print head, depending on the size of the code you need to apply.
  • Touchscreen control Codes, dates and layouts are set on the on-board touchscreen — no separate computer needed to run it.
  • Universal power Runs on 100–240 V, and ships with a choice of AU, EU, UK or US plug.
  • USB interface A USB connection for straightforward data transfer from a computer, with no network configuration.
  • Portable Around 10 kg, boxed at 66 × 40 × 20 cm — light enough to move between lines or benches.
